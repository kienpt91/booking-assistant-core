# 0006. Personal data: separate, keyed, encrypted, redacted, erasable

Status: accepted

## Decision

- `clients` holds nothing personal; `client_pii` holds the phone number and the profile name and can be deleted alone. The phone number is stored as an HMAC-SHA256 with a server-side key, computed over the salon id and the number (the lookup; the same number in two salons has two hashes), and as an AES-256-GCM envelope (to send a reply); the name as an envelope. The recipient and the text of queued replies, and the text of the conversation window, are envelopes under a second key. Every envelope is `v2:KEYID:IV:CIPHERTEXT:TAG`, with a 12 byte nonce and a 16 byte tag that are length-checked, and it authenticates a context made of the table, the column, the salon and the id of the row (`src/application/aad.ts`) and the purpose of the cipher: a value copied to another row, column, salon or purpose does not decrypt. Keys are rings with ids (`id=key,id2=key2`, the first is active) from the environment; parsing accepts only 32 bytes as hex or canonical base64 and refuses keys with fewer than 12 different byte values; the three keys must differ. The database never sees a key.
- Numbers are normalised to E.164 (Arabic-Indic digits and full-width forms included) before hashing, so the usual spellings of a number are one client. A number written with a `(0)` trunk digit is refused instead of becoming a second client, because which digit is the trunk digit depends on the country.
- Logs and audit arguments go through `redactValue`: sensitive keys are replaced, strings lose e-mail addresses, card-like and phone-like numbers (UUIDs and ISO dates survive). The conversation window stores text after the same masking. Error messages are fixed sentences keyed by a code; none carries data.
- Erasure (`eraseClient`, `eraseClientByPhone`): deletes the personal data, the conversation, queued replies, pending proposals and stored answers; cancels future appointments; removes the notes; keeps the shape of the history under a client id that points to nobody, and the audit log (which holds no personal data). Retention (`applyRetention`) deletes old conversation text, sent replies, message ids, stored answers, proposals and audit rows.

## Known gaps (stated, not hidden)

Appointment notes are free text: they are masked for phone numbers, e-mail addresses and card-like numbers when they are written (so also in the stored answer of a retry), but a name or a health detail in a note is stored unencrypted until erasure or retention removes it. The prompt carries the client's name, their messages and their notes to the model provider. The audit log is pseudonymous (client id, tool, time), not anonymous, and survives erasure. `test/integration/adapter.pg.test.ts` scans every text column of the database after a full conversation for the number, the e-mail address and the name and requires none of them anywhere; it also requires the outbox and conversation columns to be sealed. If your threat model needs more, encrypt the notes in the repositories with the message cipher.
